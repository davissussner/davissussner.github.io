# davissussner.github.io

- [Mascot Mania](https://davissussner.github.io/mascot-mania/): play as Herbie Husker and fight your way through every Big Ten mascot.
- [Uber Roulette](https://davissussner.github.io/uber-roulette/): pick which roommate orders the Uber to the bar with a marble race, horse race, human catapult, bear chase, pass the beer or wheel spin. Second to last takes a shot.
- [Class Picker](https://davissussner.github.io/class-picker/): pick a student at random with a balloon pop, wheel spin, marble race or horse race. Handles a full class, with fair turns so nobody goes twice until everyone has.
